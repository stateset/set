# Security Policy

## Supported releases

Security fixes are provided for the latest `0.3.x` release. Older prerelease lines should be
upgraded before reporting compatibility-specific issues.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue. Send the report to `security@setchain.io` with:

- the affected release, commit, contract, or service;
- impact and prerequisites;
- reproducible steps or a proof of concept;
- suggested mitigations, if known;
- a safe way to contact you.

Never include live private keys, customer information, or funds in a proof of concept. Use local
Anvil accounts and synthetic data.

We aim to acknowledge reports within two business days, provide an initial severity assessment
within five business days, and send progress updates at least weekly until resolution. Disclosure
timing is coordinated with the reporter after a fix is available. Good-faith research that avoids
privacy violations, service disruption, social engineering, and movement of funds will not be
subject to legal action by the project.

Operational incidents that are not vulnerabilities should follow `docs/runbook.md`.
